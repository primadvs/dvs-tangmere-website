About Us

A brokerage of working pilots, based in Guernsey.

Pilots, Not Just Brokers

Every transaction runs through people who hold type ratings, not just sales targets. Chris Edwards flew for the RAF for two decades before moving into business aviation; James Hughes and Will Fanshawe operate rotary programmes across Northern Europe and the US. That means inspections carried out before purchase, maintenance history and squawks get read the way an operator reads them, not the way a listing agent does.

Discretion, from Guernsey

We work from Norman House on Guernsey's South Esplanade, inside the Bailiwick's established structures for high value, cross border asset transactions. Consultation, marketing, negotiation and delivery are handled discreetly and directly. No call centre, no anonymous inbox, just a named pilot on the other end of the line.

Tangmere Aircraft Sales is a brokerage based in Guernsey, built around people who fly, not just sell. The team behind every transaction holds real type ratings and real flight hours across the Royal Air Force, corporate helicopter operations and business aviation, so the advice you get is grounded in the cockpit, not a script.
